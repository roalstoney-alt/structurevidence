Review protocol

Submissions do not change state automatically.

Send a public source or counter-evidence against a specific case and statement. The original message and sender identity stay private unless separate permission is obtained.

Private receipt → human review

A reviewer checks source access, attribution, scope, date, independence, and whether the record qualifies for the stated claim. A submitted link is not accepted evidence by itself.

Public outcome, when approved

A later entry may record accepted evidence, a non-qualifying source, an access restriction, a correction, or a superseding state. Silence or a failed search never proves the underlying claim false.
