Privacy

Paid Pilot Privacy Boundary

StructEvidence intends to collect only the minimum customer information needed to accept, verify and fulfill research orders.

Order Data

Expected paid-pilot data is limited to customer email, order ID, selected product/entity, payment transaction hash where applicable, and fulfillment status.

Not Required

StructEvidence does not need a customer's wallet private key, seed phrase, exchange password or trading credentials. Search demand logging, accounts and identity analytics remain disabled until separately implemented and disclosed.

Payment Records

If USDT settlement is activated, on-chain transaction identifiers may be retained as order and accounting evidence. Public blockchain records are inherently visible outside StructEvidence.
