CML / Method pilot v0.1

Evidence before equivalence.

CML structures technical supply-chain decisions without converting uncertainty into unsupported compatibility claims.

01

Identity

Preserve manufacturer spelling and exact part-number punctuation. Similar strings are not silently merged.

02

Event

Record what changed with separate effective and knowledge clocks and an explicit event domain.

03

Dependency

Map the assembly, interface, process and customer scope that make migration consequential.

04

Evidence

Separate facts, inferences, engineering judgment, recommendations and unknowns.

05

Alternative

An identified candidate is not a qualified replacement. OEM status is modeled separately.

06

Verification

Compatibility is dimension-based and qualification is always bound to a defined scope.

07

Decision

Output defensible actions such as monitor, lifetime buy, qualify, redesign or insufficient evidence.

Frozen laws

What the method will not do

Paper compatibility != qualified replacement

40 GHz connector != 40 GHz assembly qualification

Distributor inventory != manufacturer lifecycle

Hash integrity != source truth

Missing evidence != PASS

CML decisions != predictions
