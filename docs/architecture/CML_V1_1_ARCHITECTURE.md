# CML v1.1 architecture

Method version: `CML_v1.1`  
Transition base: `a1bb8e442316c48acb0af2aca0c8c269b00340af`  
Domain: StructEvidence `TECHNICAL_RISK`

## Method identity

CML v1.1 is one method composed of Technology Transition Intelligence, Path Dependency Research, Migration Intelligence and Opportunity Discovery. It remains inside the existing StructEvidence Technical Risk domain and reuses the existing Evidence Core envelope. It does not create a parallel evidence, provenance, verification or publication system.

The primary research object for the next implementation task is the `PDRE_RECORD`. A Path Dependency Release Event occurs when a new technology, manufacturing method, material, standard, control architecture or industrial capability becomes sufficiently mature that a previously difficult-to-avoid dependency is no longer the only commercially viable technical path. Technology existence alone is not a PDRE; a new viable path must exist.

## Active research chain

```text
CURRENT DEPENDENCY
→ PATH DEPENDENCY
→ STRUCTURAL CONSTRAINT
→ ALTERNATIVE TECHNICAL PATH
→ PDRE
→ MIGRATION READINESS
→ ADOPTION FRICTION
→ STRUCTURAL EXPOSURE + BENEFICIARY MAP
→ REAL-WORLD RESPONSE
→ MARKET VALIDATION
```

Structural exposure identifies who remains dependent on the old path. The beneficiary map identifies who gains economic or strategic optionality from the new path. They are independent objects and must never be collapsed. Migration readiness and adoption friction are likewise independent: a technically mature alternative may coexist with extreme adoption friction.

## Normative laws

- Declared intent is not structural fact.
- Technical advantage is not commercial migration.
- No observable migration is not management failure.
- An affected entity is not automatically a paying customer.
- Structural exposure is not a beneficiary map.
- Research success is not commercial success.
- Public knowledge builds reputation; technical and commercial execution creates revenue.
- Human and management responses are narrative evidence, not primary structural facts.
- No human response is required to keep public-evidence research active.
- No opaque aggregate opportunity or risk score is permitted.

Research asks what dependency exists, what would prove its release, who remains exposed, and who gains if the new path succeeds. The preferred commercial path starts with beneficiaries able to act on new optionality. Trigger-based targeted outreach may follow structural and beneficiary analysis; indiscriminate cold outreach is not the default.

## Historical boundary

CML v0.1, Opportunity Validation Phases A, B and B-R1, and the Phase C0 Human Discovery framework are `HISTORICAL_METHOD_BRANCH` records. C0 is additionally `DEPRECATED_AS_PRIMARY_RESEARCH_GATE`. These branches remain valid method history and evidence about limited discovery paths. Their findings, clocks, hashes, release states, schemas, tests and routes are unchanged.
