# CML Unified Site Architecture v0.1a

StructEvidence operates one brand, repository, Evidence Core, record store, Verify surface and release-governance system. `structurevidence.org` is the canonical research and application domain. `structevidence.com` is the acquisition domain; its root uses `landing.html` while shared paths proxy the `.org` artifacts. Structural Intelligence and Technical Risk / CML are peer evidence domains, not separate products or truth systems.
