# Phase 4A Access Boundary v0.1

Public: State, Change, public Evidence, public Branches.

Invite-only: Founding Research Room and public-case discussion.

Protected customer/private: Research Requests, decision context, Outcomes, contact details, pricing, quotes, payment intent, and manual invoice references.

Internal only: Demand Signals, public-author targeting lists, candidate scores, contact recommendations, draft outreach, review notes, attribution, and outreach status.

Discord may show public State/Change/Evidence summaries. It must not store full private Requests, email, commercial context, payment information, or private Outcomes. Runtime DSR data is stored under ignored `data/internal/` or protected database tables and is never included in public responses, metadata, sitemap, or telemetry.
