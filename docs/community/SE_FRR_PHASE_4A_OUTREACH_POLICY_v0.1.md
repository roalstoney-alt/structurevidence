# Phase 4A Outreach Policy v0.1

Every external action requires human review and manual execution. Automated follow, reply, DM, invite, email, and advertising are prohibited.

Value-first order: relevant observation → relevant State/Evidence boundary → useful public response → conversation → optional Research Room invitation. Prefer a public reply when appropriate. Drafts must reference the actual problem, specific State, evidence boundary, and why the chain may help.

Initial volume is capped at 3–5 contacts before review of signal quality, message relevance, State usefulness, contact method, and whether expansion to 20 contacts is approved. Spam complaints, platform-policy uncertainty, private-data leakage, excessive bot permissions, unreliable State links, or canonical integrity failure pause acquisition immediately.

Platform public-access and automation rules control. Unsupported automation is recorded as `MANUAL_SEARCH_REQUIRED`; no scraping bypass is permitted.
