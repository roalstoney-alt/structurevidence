## Allocation method

Each card maps one candidate to its current repository state, linked decision, proceed-without-research test, minimum new evidence, minimum source class, research scope, qualitative information gain, decision impact, reuse, commercial relevance, unknown costs, future stop condition, Codex recommendation, and pending human decision.

No numeric value, priority, commercial, or research score is generated. Recommendations do not change CML state or authorize expenditure.
