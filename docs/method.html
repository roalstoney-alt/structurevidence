Method

Structural Dynamics asks what appears to be changing in a system. Evidence Dynamics asks whether public claims and observations about that system can be reconciled.

Hierarchy

RDL = Research governance

RTP = Provenance / history / identity

ECN = Evidence contribution infrastructure

Structural Dynamics = Reality/system structure research

Evidence Dynamics = Public evidence research

ECL = Evidence Consistency Layer

Pipeline

Public Artifact -> Claim / Observation -> Entity Resolution -> Time / Scope Alignment -> Source Dependency -> Numerical Reconciliation -> Semantic Analysis -> Competing Hypotheses -> Counter-Evidence Search -> Research Finding

Permanent Method Laws

PUBLIC CLAIM != REALITY

ARTIFACT INTEGRITY != CONTENT TRUTH

MULTIPLE URLs != INDEPENDENT SOURCES

HOSTING INDEPENDENCE != EVIDENCE INDEPENDENCE

CHANGE != CONTRADICTION

INCONSISTENCY != FALSEHOOD

OBSERVED CONTRADICTION != POTENTIAL FALSIFIER

APPARENT CONFLICT != MATERIAL INCONSISTENCY

Correction Policy

Original reports remain preserved. Corrections are appended. Superseded findings remain traceable.

Research only. No investment advice. Evidence inconsistency does not imply falsehood, misconduct or fraud. All findings are subject to correction and supersession.
