# SE-NO-RATING-NO-DECISION-001
Status: USER-APPROVED PRINCIPLE. Applies to all StructEvidence tracker, case, challenge, digest, commercial and pilot surfaces.
Date: 2026-10-26

## Hard boundary
StructEvidence makes no investment, procurement, strategy or technical route recommendation; offers no scores, ratings, rankings, bullish/bearish labels, price targets, comparative winner lists, or implied directional signals. Do not synthesize those equivalents through colors, badges, sentiment, ordering, a composite confidence index, traffic-light indicators or weighted 'risk' grades.

We can nevertheless perform auditable **rule-based evidence qualification**: whether a specific source meets the published acceptance conditions for a specific claim at a dated cutoff. This is not a judgment on a company's overall quality, truthfulness, management, prospects or security valuation.

## Required three-layer separation
1. SOURCE ATTRIBUTION: Who said what, when, with which accessible material and provenance? Attribution does not certify truth.
2. EVIDENCE CONDITION: What is supported, contradicted, unestablished, inaccessible or still awaiting evidence *within stated scope and rules*? Expose each rule, missing facet, dissent and update reason; never output scalar or categorical company ratings.
3. CLIENT DECISION: Client-owned action, exposure, weights and thresholds; UNSPECIFIED unless genuinely supplied by an authorized client. SE cannot choose, infer or suggest proceed/hold/exit.

## Public neutral wording
Use 'issuer reported', 'according to filing X', 'not established within reviewed documents', 'awaiting source verification', 'new evidence appended at cutoff'. Never turn 'NOT_ESTABLISHED' into 'false', a trading signal or a moral criticism. Avoid headlines implying scandal or vindication. No preferential evidence treatment for companies, readers, sponsors or critics. Open corrections and challenges remain append-only and subjected to the same rules.

## Acceptance tests
Guard UI, generated summaries, notifications, weekly digests, social drafts, alt text and machine outputs against direct and indirect ratings and decision recommendations. Numeric counts, dates, monetary reported amounts and exact rule thresholds remain permitted as descriptive data, never combined into a judgement score. Record counterexamples of rhetorical or visual proxy ratings. Do not rewrite original evidence or case state.

No merge, deploy, publishing, paid tiers, customer outreach, or automation authorized by this file.
