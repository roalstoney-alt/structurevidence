# SE-DECISION-BOUNDARY-001 — Evidence Evolves. Decisions Remain Accountable.
Status: USER-APPROVED PRODUCT PRINCIPLE / ENGINEERING ACCEPTANCE BASELINE
Date: 2026-10-25
Project: SE-DUAL-SITE-UPGRADE-002; Phase B; PR #49

## Canonical commitments
- Verify Your Verify — Lower the cost of knowing what you can trust.
- We can be wrong. We must remain auditable.
- StructEvidence does not sell authoritative conclusions. It preserves how evidence evolves from an initial observation into a time-bounded, challengeable decision boundary.
- A decision belongs to the customer. The evidence history remains auditable.

## Product contract
Every citable boundary must distinguish (1) scoped claim/decision question, (2) original observation and evidence lineage, (3) review scope and inaccessible sources, (4) evidence cutoff vs last check, (5) current support/contradiction/unknown, (6) acceptance and closure conditions, (7) provenance, review and method versions, (8) append-only changes, and (9) consequences for particular decision types WITHOUT issuing a decision recommendation.

Evidence state is not truth. A qualified source is not a correct investment or purchase. Unknown does not mean false; no qualifying record in a bounded search does not mean no record exists. Disagreement is evidence about the review process and must remain visible.

## Public (.org) and private (.com)
.org publishes challengeable public records, stable IDs, source dependency views, gap closure tests and visible corrections. Public challenges can be anonymous/pseudonymous, must cite a precise claim or row, and may not auto-change status. Do not demand expert endorsement or crowd votes as an authority substitute.
.com offers client-specific decision boundary records and longitudinal watch/reverification, with actual availability/prices accurately labeled. Scope, decision action, exposure and thresholds belong to the client; SE does not claim savings without measured customer data. This Phase B change does not authorize .com deployment.

## Challenge conditions
- Types: provenance/lineage, evidence-level, missing counterevidence, rule applicability, temporal error.
- Append-only moderated events: submitted, validated/declined with reason, reviewed, reclassified (if justified), closed.
- Do not leak private submitter information or raw unmoderated abuse. Expertise claims remain UNVERIFIED absent independent proof; submitter authority is never evidence qualification.
- No fictional submission button or public intake without a functioning secure backend, rate limiting, moderation and privacy checks.
