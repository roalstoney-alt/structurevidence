# SE-EVIDENCE-STRUCTURE-NEUTRALITY-001 — Source-grounded structure, not authority
Status: USER-APPROVED CORE PRINCIPLE
Applies to: StructEvidence public research, IREN tracker pilot, case change feeds and private decision-boundary outputs.
Date: 2026-10-26

## Canonical framing
StructEvidence does not position itself as an authoritative or intrinsically trustworthy source of facts, a critic of source organizations, or an arbiter of corporate decisions. It uses attributable publicly accessible source materials to organize the evolving structure of claims, evidence, unknowns, relationships and explicit decision conditions. Additional evidence, corrections and direct contributions from originating parties may improve the structure's correspondence to reality, without ever making it final or infallible.

Reader-facing short form:
“We don't ask you to trust us instead of the sources. We show what the sources say, what they establish within a stated scope, what remains unknown, and how the record changes when new evidence arrives.”

“Verify Your Verify — Lower the cost of knowing what you can trust.”
“We can be wrong. We must remain auditable.”

## Non-authority contract
- **Source is the subject of attribution, not the guarantee of truth.** Record exact source, issuer and author lineage, access, capture time, cutoff, methodology, legal-disclosure context, retractions and contradictions.
- **StructEvidence is a transparent organizer**, not an independent witness, a professional auditor or a certification agency merely because it produced a structured record. Never imply that our formatting, hashing, AI classification, or a legal filing establishes the actual operating outcome.
- **No oppositional default.** Unknown/NOT_ESTABLISHED marks the review boundary, not that an issuer lied, a project failed, an investment was unwise, or evidence globally does not exist. It is not a bearish signal.
- **No positive default.** Company filings and press releases support what the issuer disclosed (and sometimes independently audited financial statement particulars), not every asserted operational claim or promised outcome.
- **Open to source parties.** Originators and knowledgeable external parties can submit additional material or a correction. Treat all submissions as claims until verified against explicit acceptance rules; neither the issuer nor a critic gains privileged authority over state changes.
- **Append-only corrections.** Preserve earlier classifications, the reason for change, material provenance and the reviewer/automation identity and timestamp; do not conceal prior errors.
- **Client decision ownership.** The service exposes dependencies and client-selected thresholds. It does not select PROCEED/HOLD/EXIT, issue stock targets, infer liability, or speculate about alternative histories.
- **No implied professional certification.** “verified by StructEvidence” is prohibited without explicit explanation of method and bounded scope. Prefer “checked against these sources/rules as of [time]”.

## IREN tracker language and layout guards
Use “Issuer-reported”, “According to [specific SEC filing]”, “Not established within the reviewed source set”, “Would require [named additional evidence]”, “Changed since previous dated snapshot”.
Do NOT use “IREN is untrustworthy”, “We exposed the truth”, “Facts guaranteed”, “IREN's strategy is wrong”, “We certify this”, “Investors should buy/sell/avoid”, or unqualified “No evidence exists”.
The project page explains source kinds and review limitations in plain language. Each condition allows challenge and additional sources. If original source party submits material, register its attributable provenance and equal treatment; do not auto-upgrade.
Distinguish firm-reported capital commitments from customer-defined exposure and unknown internal board intentions.

## Tests and approval gates
- Copy and generated narrative guard against praise/blame, investment inference, unsupported negative inference, overstated certainty and rhetorical “gotcha” framing.
- Each material statement linked to a verifiable source record or labeled UNKNOWN/DERIVED/ILLUSTRATIVE.
- Field observations, issuer reporting, third-party testimony, audited particulars and source reprints explicitly distinguished.
- Correction/issuer-response paths maintain equal acceptance and no hidden priority.
- Existing states and frozen evidence bytes untouched. This principle does not authorize an automatic merge or production publication.

## Product hypothesis (not proved)
The value is reduced cost of understanding the *current structure of accessible evidence*, which may become more faithful with continued contributions. A pilot must measure that claim independently; neither precision-looking hashes nor many sources alone prove accuracy or customer ROI.
