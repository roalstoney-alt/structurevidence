# SE-FRR Product Language v0.1

Preferred nouns and verbs are: State, Observed, Recorded, Changed, Evidence, Counter Evidence, Unknown, Branch, History, and What was knowable.

The current-State boundary is always stated as: “This is the latest recorded StructEvidence State, not a prediction or guarantee of future outcome.”

Confidence is expressed only as:

- SUPPORTED
- NOT YET SUPPORTED
- UNKNOWN

Do not use numeric confidence, investment language, winning-technology claims, guarantees, or analyst recommendations. A Challenge is “submitted,” never “updated,” until review creates a new append-only State.

First-time explanation: StructEvidence does not overwrite old conclusions. When evidence changes, a new State is created, so both the current record and what was knowable earlier remain inspectable.
