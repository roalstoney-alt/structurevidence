# Paid-Readiness Copy Map

| Surface | Primary message | Primary CTA | Boundary language |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home | From question to defensible decision. | Start with a decision | We do not force an answer. |
| 800V case | One named deployment is observed. | Evaluate for my company | Single deployment ≠ industry adoption. |
| Verify | Verify one claim before you commit. | Request verification scope | Submission does not authorize research. |
| Decision Pack | Turn evidence into an actionable decision package. | Build my decision pack | The pack does not make the decision. |
| Context | Start with your decision, not a blank research brief. | Submit decision context | Public and private evidence stay separated. |

Required states are rendered verbatim where applicable: `SUPPORTED`, `CONTRADICTED`, `UNKNOWN`, `NOT ESTABLISHED`, `OBSERVED`, and `PENDING VERIFICATION`.
