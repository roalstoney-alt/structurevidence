## Page roles

- `/`: commercial proposition, live proof, bounded-process evidence, progressive services, deliverable previews, method trust.
- `/cases/800vdc/`: current evidence state, decision transition, provenance, counter-evidence, dependency transfer, subsystem watch, unresolved gaps, customer-application CTA.
- `/verify/`: decision-critical claim intake, verification-record preview, pre-scope versus paid-work boundary.
- `/decision-pack/`: complete deliverable structure, generic use cases, context CTA.
- `/context/`: company decision intake, response states, public/private evidence boundary.

Existing monitor, technical-risk, method, research, report, policy, and historical artifact routes are preserved.
