Service Privacy Boundary

MATRIX ASIA PACIFIC LIMITED operates StructEvidence and collects only information reasonably needed to review, scope, contract for, deliver and support a requested research service.

What we collect and why

Required intake fields identify a reply address and the decision or claim to review. Name, organization, technical object, deadline and bounded existing-evidence context may be optional depending on the form. If required information is not provided, we cannot record or scope the request.

Do not submit patient records, identity documents, passwords, private keys, full customer databases, or unauthorized trade secrets. Initial scoping does not require them. Material that appears unsuitable for ordinary handling may be isolated for human review; no automated filter detects every sensitive item.

Recipients and separation

Authorized StructEvidence personnel and service providers needed for hosting, security, communication, payment/accounting or delivery may process relevant data under their applicable arrangements. Customer submissions remain separate from public research. Submission does not authorize research, marketing, publication, or inclusion in a public case. Any public use requires a separate, specific and verifiable authorization.

Retention and security

Records are retained only for as long as reasonably needed for scoping, service administration, accounting, disputes, security and applicable legal duties. Exact provider, backup and retention schedules are under operator review and are not represented here as automatic deletion guarantees. Administrative access is protected separately from public intake. Public responses and ordinary error logs must not reproduce submitted narrative.

Your requests

To request access, correction, restriction or other applicable handling, email support@structevidence.com with the relevant request identifier and the minimum information needed to locate the record. Do not send identity documents unless specifically and securely requested after review.

Payment records

Invoices and transaction identifiers may be retained as order and accounting evidence. Public blockchain records, where a digital-asset settlement option is expressly agreed, are visible outside StructEvidence.
