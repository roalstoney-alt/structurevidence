Provenance layer

RTP: Research Traceability Protocol

Records who acted, what artifact was used, where a result came from, what transformation occurred and whether the chain can be reproduced.

Role in system

Provenance layer. Positioned inside RDL -> RTP -> [Structural Dynamics -> MDL] + [Evidence Dynamics -> ECL] -> ECN.
