Refund Policy

Manual Service Refund Boundary

Scope, price, delivery timing, evidence cut-off, third-party costs and cancellation terms are confirmed in writing before work begins.

Before and after work begins

Before work begins, cancellation follows the accepted written scope. After work begins, fees are generally non-refundable to the extent reserved research capacity and agreed computational or third-party costs have been consumed. This does not remove rights that apply by law.

Reviewable exceptions

Duplicate payment, failure to deliver the agreed scope, confirmed material service error, an express written exception, or applicable legal rights are reviewed individually. Correction of a confirmed StructEvidence error is not conditional on purchase of a new evidence cycle.

Later evidence and rush handling

New evidence after the delivery cut-off may require a new paid scope; that is different from correcting our own error. Rush handling costs USD 399 per request when capacity and terms are accepted in writing and never bypasses evidence, privacy or authorization controls.

Settlement exceptions

Any optional digital-asset settlement is reviewed manually. Underpayment, overpayment, wrong-token and wrong-network issues depend on the agreed route and recoverability; network or third-party costs are identified where applicable. Refunds are not automatically redirected to a newly supplied third-party address.

Contact

Send the request identifier and minimum relevant context to support@structevidence.com. Do not send passwords, private keys, identity documents or unrelated confidential material.
