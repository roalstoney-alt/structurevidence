# StructEvidence Monitoring Product Contract v1.0

StructEvidence monitors how events and evidence alter digital-asset market structure and liquidity. Every published state transition is snapshot-bound, hash-verifiable, and replayable through its recorded lineage.

## Boundaries

- Monitoring is not price prediction.
- Artifact integrity is not content truth.
- Source authority is not market relevance.
- Epistemic status is independent from market impact.
- Observation is not causation.
- Structural Level is distinct from structural Delta.
- Currentness is not truth.
- A snapshot export is not live monitoring.

Unverified, contested, false, corrected, and retracted information may remain in the event ledger when it is part of the observable information environment. Recording it does not endorse it. Market effects are reported only when measured; otherwise the state is `NOT_MEASURED`.

The system does not emit price targets, rankings, scores, buy/sell/hold instructions, or trade timing.
