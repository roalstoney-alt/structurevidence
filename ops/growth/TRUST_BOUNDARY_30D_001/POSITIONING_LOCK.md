Campaign window: 2026-10-08 through 2026-11-06 (Asia/Shanghai)

Mode: Controlled market validation

Primary public channel: X

## Category thesis

Generation created the need for verification. Verification creates the need for trust boundaries.

UniPat asks whether an AI performed correctly under an evaluation. StructEvidence asks what makes that evaluation representative of reality and where trust stops.

## Frozen hooks

- Decision maker, Chinese: 验证过的结果，仍然不等于事实。
- Decision maker, English: A verified result is still not a fact.
- Investor, Chinese: 谁来验证验证者？
- Investor, English: Who verifies the verifier?

## Product definition

StructEvidence records what evidence supports a result, what it does not support, what remains unknown, the knowledge cutoff, and what new observation should reopen the conclusion.

## Public content boundary

Every public item must contain one question, one boundary, and one evidence link or one frozen one-pager. The campaign must not present StructEvidence as AI research, due diligence automation, fact checking, investment scoring, or another verification model.

Content must be rejected before publication if it contains or implies any of the following:

- guaranteed
- proven true
- investment recommendation
- approval basis
- investment grade
- legal assurance
- we replace due diligence
- best technology
- safe investment
- UniPat is wrong
- experts cannot be trusted

Critique the epistemic boundary, not another company. Do not change this positioning in response to likes, views, or impressions.
