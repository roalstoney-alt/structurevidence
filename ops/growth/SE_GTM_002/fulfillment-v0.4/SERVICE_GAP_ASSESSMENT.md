## Required assessment already defined by the Order

The FTC Order defines a substantial assurance engagement. The approved assessor
must independently evaluate program implementation and effectiveness, identify
gaps or material noncompliance, trace evidence, and avoid primary reliance on
management assertions. StructEvidence must not represent itself as that assessor
without meeting the Order's qualifications, independence requirements and FTC
approval process.

## Potential ancillary task

A legitimate ancillary task could be a time-indexed evidence map for safeguards
that changed during the assessment period:

- map each safeguard revision to its effective interval;
- preserve the evidence available before and after each revision;
- link documents, tests and interviews without evaluating privileged content;
- record unresolved evidence gaps and later closure evidence append-only;
- export an assessor-controlled evidence index.

This could support the Order's requirement that revised or added safeguards be
assessed for the period in which each was effective. It would not replace the
assessor, issue an assurance opinion, certify compliance, perform penetration
testing, determine legal sufficiency or communicate with the FTC.
