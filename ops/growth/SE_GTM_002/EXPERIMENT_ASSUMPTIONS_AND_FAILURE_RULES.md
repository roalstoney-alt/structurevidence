# SE-GTM-002 assumptions and failure rules

## Hypotheses

1. A specific, evidence-backed decision blocker may outperform generic
   company-level prospecting.
2. A partner with existing customer relationships may surface qualified demand
   more efficiently than direct cold outreach.

Neither hypothesis is established.

## Required separation

- Observed fact: directly supported by a cited public source or confirmed
  customer statement.
- Inference: a bounded interpretation derived from observed facts.
- Hypothesis: a proposition the pilot is testing.
- Confirmed customer statement: recorded only after an attributable customer
  interaction.

Never convert one class into another.

## Qualification gates

A direct candidate is qualified only when all of the following are true:

1. Source is traceable.
2. Affected company is named.
3. An active decision is evidenced.
4. Missing fact is material to that decision.
5. Decision-owner role is identified.
6. Contact route is verified.
7. Route purpose fit is DIRECT or ROUTABLE.

The numeric score cannot waive any gate. Sending additionally requires explicit
human approval for one recipient, one route and one message.

## Score

Five components are scored from 0 to 20:

- evidence traceability;
- decision pressure;
- material gap;
- owner and route;
- deliverable fit.

`candidate_score` must equal their sum. It is a review-order aid only.

## Stop rules

- Insufficient evidence: research further or stop.
- No named affected company or active decision: do not qualify.
- No verified purpose-fit route: do not send.
- Contradictory evidence: preserve it and reopen review.
- No approved external research budget: incur no external cost.
- No explicit human send authorization: draft only.
- Silence: retain as no observation, never as absent demand.
- No actual response or payment evidence: do not claim acquisition success.

## Failure conditions

The direct hypothesis is not supported if reviewed candidates repeatedly expose
technical triggers but no named active buyer decision can be established.

The partner hypothesis is not supported if purpose-fit partners decline,
cannot route the proposition to the relevant function, or produce no qualified
decision context.

Production functionality, historical research, Stop-Points and existing growth
campaigns are outside this pilot and must not be altered to improve results.
