# DPAL v0.3 counterevidence and failures

## Cross-channel counterevidence

- A technology lifecycle event identifies a vendor deadline, not a named
  customer decision.
- A regulatory order identifies an obligation, not an open procurement.
- A required independent assessment may already have an incumbent provider.
- An investor, support or recall route is not a purpose-fit route to the
  accountable buyer.
- A public accountable role is not a confirmed buyer.
- A past warning-letter response window may have been superseded by non-public
  or later regulatory interaction.
- Existing auditors, regulator processes and technical-assessment firms may
  already cover the proposed scope.
- No response, referral, scope request, budget or payment has been observed.

## Candidate-specific stops

- Microsoft and Siemens lifecycle candidates: HOLD because no named deployer.
- Hyundai and FCA recalls: HOLD because the remedy process is established and
  no separate independent-verification need or purpose-fit commercial route is
  evidenced.
- TTI/TE PCN: REOPEN freshness and downstream customer state before any further
  qualification.
- GoDaddy and Marriott: RESEARCH FURTHER only; an independent assessor is
  required, but open scope and incumbent status are unknown.
- Super Micro and ADTRAN: HOLD; current pressure is verified, while existing
  audit/attestation work is strong counterevidence to an unfilled scope.
- Apotex: REOPEN because FDA status and assessor engagement may have changed.
- Apple and Meta: HOLD/REOPEN because appeal or later compliance assessment can
  supersede the 2025 state.
- Wisconsin Pharmacal and AH USA: REOPEN because response windows passed and
  current regulatory status is not established.
- Workado: HOLD because the final order requires evidence retention and reports,
  but does not establish an independent-verification procurement.

## Experiment failure rule

Do not claim DPAL success from a higher score or stronger public obligation.
Success requires a qualified CDSP record and later human-observed demand. This
run has neither.
