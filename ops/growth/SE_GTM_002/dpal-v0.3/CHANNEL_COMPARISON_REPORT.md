## Observed channel result

| Channel | Records reviewed | Verified pressure events | Named organizations | Confirmed external-verification need | Qualified CDSP | Confirmed buyer | External cost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Technology-triggered direct discovery | 6 | 6 | 3 | 0 | 0 | 0 | USD 0 |
| Audit/risk-triggered direct discovery | 5 | 5 | 5 | 3 | 0 | 0 | USD 0 |
| Regulatory/policy-triggered direct discovery | 5 | 5 | 5 | 0 | 0 | 0 | USD 0 |
| Partner-referred opportunities | 0 referrals | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | USD 0 |

The audit/risk cohort produced the strongest evidence of an externally imposed
verification obligation. It still produced no qualified commercial opportunity:
none of the public records establishes an open procurement, an available scope
not already served by an incumbent, and a purpose-fit route to the accountable
buyer.

Technology events remain weak acquisition signals when the deployed customer
is unknown. Vehicle recalls improve organization and accountability evidence,
but they also show mature regulator and remedy processes that may leave no
unfilled StructEvidence role.

Policy and warning-letter events often identify an obligation but are highly
sensitive to freshness. A past response deadline is not evidence of a current
buying decision.

No partner has referred an opportunity. The partner channel therefore remains
a hypothesis and must not be compared as if referrals had occurred.

## Consolidated partner-fit research

This completes the unfinished fit questions from the superseded v0.2 planning
instruction without changing the original partner records.

### Rochester Electronics — complementary-gap research

Official material supports authorized distribution, licensed manufacturing,
design/replication, test, reliability and lifecycle planning for obsolete
semiconductors. Public materials also address redesign and requalification
costs. A possible complement is a buyer-side, time-bounded record of why the
organization selected continued supply, replication or redesign and what
evidence would reopen that decision.

That complement is only a hypothesis. The reviewed pages do not establish that
Rochester lacks this capability, wants a partner, or has a specific customer
opportunity. `STATUS = RESEARCH_ONLY_HOLD`.

### TTI — complementary-gap research

TTI's public material supports EOL notification, affected-customer routing and
alternative/lifetime-buy support. TE Connectivity PCN P-25-027928 provides a
concrete example: TTI is named as a past customer/distributor and is asked to
notify affected end customers where there is no last-time buy or replacement.

A possible complement is an independent cross-supplier Stop-Point that records
which downstream continuity option was supportable at the time. The missing
facts are the named affected end customer, a currently unresolved decision and
evidence that TTI's own process leaves such a gap. `STATUS = RESEARCH_ONLY_HOLD`.

### SiliconExpert — competitive capability benchmark

SiliconExpert publicly supports part research, BOM analysis, lifecycle and
sourcing risk, compliance data, alternate identification and API/workflow
integration. This materially overlaps the proposed technology-event discovery
and evidence-input layer.

The only possible non-overlapping hypothesis is a method-neutral decision-memory
layer after component intelligence has identified risk. No public evidence
establishes demand for that separation. Treat SiliconExpert as a competitive
benchmark, not a first-wave partner. `STATUS = COMPETITIVE_HOLD`.

Sources:

- https://www.siliconexpert.com/products/
- https://www.siliconexpert.com/contact-api/

### SGS and Intertek — specific-opportunity gate

Both organizations publicly offer independent vendor assessment, technical
auditing, compliance testing, supplier capability review and follow-up. Those
capabilities overlap the strongest DPAL cases more than they complement them.

Retain both on HOLD unless a named customer decision shows a pre-audit evidence
boundary that their published audit scope does not already cover. No such
opportunity is supported in this run.

Sources:

- https://www.sgs.com/en/services/vendor-assessment-and-technical-audit
- https://www.intertek.com/technical-inspection/auditing/

## Comparative conclusion

`AUDIT_RISK_TRIGGERED` is the best research-order channel, not a validated
acquisition channel. Direct public discovery has produced zero qualified buyers;
partner-referred discovery has produced zero referrals. The next experiment
must preserve these as separate denominators.
