## Bounded finding

There is public evidence that frontier-model organizations use data
partnerships, external evaluations and specialist benchmark collaborators.
There is no public evidence that any reviewed organization has requested the
specific Temporal Evidence Challenge Pack built in this pilot.

The pilot therefore establishes a testable product and a small set of possible
routes. It does not establish demand, a budget, a buyer, a procurement event or
a vendor relationship.

## UniPat AI

### Public capability

UniPat publicly presents a portfolio spanning fresh software-engineering tasks,
scientific reproduction, long-horizon SaaS workflows, prediction and expert
judgment. Its official benchmark index and product pages support the capability
descriptions used in the competitive matrix.

- benchmark index: https://unipat.ai/benchmarks
- Monthly-SWEBench: https://unipat.ai/benchmarks/MonthlySWEBench
- PaperBenchX: https://unipat.ai/blog/PaperBenchX
- SaaS-Bench: https://unipat.ai/blog/SaaS-Bench
- Echo: https://unipat.ai/blog/Echo
- ExpertEval: https://unipat.ai/blog/ExpertEval

The company publishes `contact@unipat.ai`. PaperBenchX also publishes
`paperbenchx@unipat.ai` for collaboration. These are attributable routes, but
they do not establish that UniPat is buying third-party temporal evidence
datasets.

### Reported order amount

`UNIPAT_ORDER_AMOUNT_STATE = REPORTED_NOT_COMPANY_CONFIRMED`

Dealroom reported that UniPat had about USD 30 million of customer orders at
the end of May 2026, citing a person familiar with the matter. The report does
not provide a company filing, contract list or recognized-revenue figure:

https://dealroom.co/news/150873-chinas-unipat-hits-1b-valuation-on-just-30m-in-orders/

A separate Chinese report attributes approximately USD 30 million of orders to
Humanlaya, not UniPat:

https://timeline.sohu.com/news/AISWS5Zhxm?from=news

The two claims are preserved separately. Neither amount is treated as audited
revenue. No individual model developer is assigned to UniPat solely because a
report says named developers purchased from "UniPat, Humanlaya, or both."

## Humanlaya

Humanlaya's official site presents high-complexity real-world benchmarks and an
enterprise-data partnership proposition. It publishes the route
`humanlaya-enterprise@humanlaya.com` for organizations that may contribute
enterprise workflows:

https://humanlaya.com/

This supports the existence of a collaboration/intake route and a competing
expert-data capability. It does not show that Humanlaya purchases third-party
StructEvidence-style datasets or that any reported customer purchased from
Humanlaya rather than UniPat.

route.

## Unresolved commercial assumptions

- A buyer values claim-state transitions and explicit abstention enough to pay.
- An eight-task sample is large enough to qualify a larger engagement.
- Public-source temporal tasks are sufficiently differentiated from products
  already offered by UniPat, Humanlaya and other benchmark vendors.
- The buyer accepts a rights model that links to third-party sources without
  redistributing source text.
- A model developer can ingest the proposed output schema and scoring contract.
- A suitable procurement owner, budget and timing exist.

All remain `NOT_ESTABLISHED`.
