The task pack contains StructEvidence-authored normalized facts, claim IDs,
states, timestamps and links to public sources. It does not copy full articles,
paywalled material, customer data or private evidence.

The repository contains `LICENSE_PENDING.md`; this pilot has no separate owner
or counsel determination authorizing commercial redistribution of the task
pack. Public availability of source facts or repository files is not treated as
commercial licensing permission.

Before sharing the dataset externally, a human owner must confirm:

1. authority to license the StructEvidence-authored task and gold records;
2. acceptable use of third-party names, URLs and short factual paraphrases;
3. whether a buyer may train on, modify or redistribute the pack;
4. the license, attribution, warranty and update terms;
5. whether the Irkutsk public-health tasks require additional review.

Until then, only the high-level product description may be considered for a
separately authorized inquiry. The dataset itself must not be sent.
