# Source-rights policy — draft

Each publishable source review should record `rights_basis`, `public_quote_allowed`, and `redistribution_scope`. Unknown permission remains `UNKNOWN`; it must not be converted to consent. Links, short attributed descriptions and licensed assets are treated separately from republication of full articles, images, papers, customer documents or database exports.

Any reliance on quotation, fair dealing/fair use, news reporting or another exception requires human review of jurisdiction, purpose, amount and market effect. Internal quote-length controls are risk controls, not a universal statutory safe harbour.

`LICENSE_PENDING` remains unchanged for the repository. No repository-wide MIT, CC or other license is granted by this remediation. StructEvidence-authored schemas and summaries, third-party material, customer material and licensed media retain distinct rights status.
