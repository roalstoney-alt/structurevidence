# Corrections & Publication Policy — draft

Public records distinguish `CORRECTION` (our factual, citation, transcription or analysis error), `SCOPE_CLARIFICATION` (the prior wording overstated its boundary), and `EVIDENCE_ADDITION` (qualifying evidence after the prior cut-off). A confirmed StructEvidence error may be corrected without purchase of a new research cycle. A new cut-off, source universe or question may require a separately agreed scope.

Publication states are `CURRENT`, `SUPERSEDED`, `CORRECTED`, `RESTRICTED`, and `WITHDRAWN`. History is append-only by default, but public access is not promised forever. A credible privacy, copyright, falsity, safety or legal-order risk can trigger restricted publication. The old address must return a non-sensitive status notice; HTML, JSON, snapshots, feeds and caches must be considered together. `noindex` alone is not withdrawal.

Internal retention requires a documented purpose, restricted access and a review period. This task creates policy and synthetic validation only; it does not remove a real case or rewrite production history.

Complaints may be sent to `support@structevidence.com` with the URL, version, sentence, supporting evidence and requested action. Do not send identity documents, patient records or unrelated confidential material by default. An internal triage target is not a promise of resolution within a fixed public deadline.
