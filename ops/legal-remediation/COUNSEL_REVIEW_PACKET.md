## Correction, restriction and historical publication

The proposed policy preserves append-only history by default but permits controlled correction, restriction and withdrawal. It does not promise that every old version remains publicly accessible, nor that a primary-record action immediately removes caches, backups, Time Travel, email, accounting records or third-party copies. Counsel should review:

- the standard and procedure for public-interest reporting, allegations, public events, falsity risk and reasonable verification;
- notice/contact requirements before or after restriction;
- retention of a non-sensitive old-URL status notice;
- legal-hold, evidentiary and correction-history requirements;
- treatment of customer requests to access, correct, restrict or withdraw material.
