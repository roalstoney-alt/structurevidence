Scope and Evidence Boundaries

StructEvidence performs public-source structural and evidence research unless a custom scope explicitly supplies additional records. Commercial payment does not change the scientific or evidentiary standard.

Research Boundary

Reports do not guarantee completeness, do not guarantee undisclosed events are detected, and do not provide investment advice, statutory audit, credit rating, solvency opinion or legal opinion. Structural classifications are methodology-dependent and may be corrected or superseded.

Payment Boundary

Paid reports purchase research depth, refresh, organization, verification and delivery. USD pricing or optional USDT settlement does not transform a report into an investment recommendation, trading signal or regulated assurance opinion.

Evidence Boundary

Evidence inconsistency does not imply falsehood, misconduct or fraud. Multiple URLs do not automatically represent independent evidence. Tool output, including internal market-context tools, is not itself a published finding without research interpretation and publication gating.
