# Security policy

Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability, exposed credential, private customer record, or personal medical information. Send a minimal report to `support@structevidence.com` with the subject `SECURITY REPORT`.

Include the affected URL or component, reproduction steps, and impact. Do not include unnecessary personal or confidential data. Public evidence submissions belong in the issue templates or the controlled [submission form](https://structurevidence.org/cases/submit-evidence/), not in security reports.
