Corrections & Publication

Evidence history is traceable, not unconditionally permanent.

We distinguish our error, a scope clarification, and qualifying evidence added after an earlier cut-off.

Change types

CORRECTION addresses a confirmed factual, citation, transcription or analysis error. SCOPE_CLARIFICATION narrows or explains wording that could overstate the reviewed boundary. EVIDENCE_ADDITION records qualifying new material without rewriting the prior evidence state.

Publication states

Records may be CURRENT, SUPERSEDED, CORRECTED, RESTRICTED or WITHDRAWN. History is appended by default, but credible privacy, copyright, falsity, safety or legal-order risk can require restricted access. An old address should show a non-sensitive status notice. HTML, JSON, snapshots, stop cards, feeds and caches must be considered together; noindex alone is not withdrawal.

Requests

Email support@structevidence.com with the URL, version, exact sentence, supporting evidence and requested action. Do not send identity documents, patient records or unrelated confidential material unless a secure route is arranged after review.

Commercial boundary

A confirmed StructEvidence service error may be corrected without purchase of a new cycle. A new cut-off, new source universe or new question may be separately scoped. Correction does not guarantee unlimited rework.
