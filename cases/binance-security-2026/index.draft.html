Decision-evidence gap · BSEC-DG001

Does public evidence establish post-incident liability coverage and completed compensation?

Status: OPEN — insufficient public evidence.

  1. Capture and hash the original pre-incident reserve snapshot, Merkle root and address bundle.
  2. Reconcile outflows, freezes, rescues, recoveries, returns and compensation without double counting.
  3. Capture the promised post-remediation proof of reserves and technical post-mortem.
  4. Add independently reviewable liability scope and exclusions.
  5. Record completed user restoration rather than relying on a promise.

No solvency, insolvency, safety, negligence, criminal or actor conclusion follows from the open state.
