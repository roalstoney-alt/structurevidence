## LinkedIn candidate

Security-incident numbers often converge before their meanings do.

For the October 29–30 Binance incident, the reviewed sources use materially different boundaries: Binance reported approximately $6.3B of impact and approximately $3B frozen; TRM and Bitquery published transaction-based estimates near $6.3B while differing on chain count and reporting about $2.7B frozen under their methods. A public Ethereum query verifies one successful transaction and its block time, but it does not establish root cause, total loss or actor identity.

The draft therefore separates operator claims, public-ledger observations, analytic inference, secondary reporting and unresolved attribution. It also treats a compensation promise as distinct from completed compensation and makes no solvency conclusion.
