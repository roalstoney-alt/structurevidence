IREN Limited: AI Cloud transition, without collapsing the evidence chain.

Which milestones are supported by dated issuer disclosures—and where do contract value, delivery, customer acceptance, ARR, recognized revenue, cash collection, and economic returns remain distinct?

30-second read

A contract, an acceptance statement, revenue, and cash are four different observations.

The sources support a $9.7bn headline Microsoft contract value, an IREN-authored statement that Horizon 1 was accepted, and $128.8m of FY26 AI Cloud revenue across IREN’s customer base. They do not establish that the headline value became recognized revenue or that it was collected in cash.
