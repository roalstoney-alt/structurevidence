Vantage reserves Heron 800 VDC SST manufacturing capacity

Event and publication: 21 October 2026 · Retrieved: 22 October 2026 · Classification: NEW_EVENT + NO_STATE_CHANGE.

Verified statement

Vantage and Heron jointly announced a strategic manufacturing-capacity agreement. Vantage says it secured more than 1 GW of manufacturing capacity for Heron Link solid-state transformers intended for future hyperscale data-center campuses.

Evidence boundary

This is a named operator-to-supplier future capacity commitment. The release does not identify a commissioned campus, shipped units, acceptance tests, operating hours, price, or independently measured field performance. Heron separately says mass production at its first large-scale factory is planned for late 2027. The accepted single-instance deployment stop therefore does not advance.
