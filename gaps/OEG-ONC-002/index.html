CLAIM

Ivonescimab with pemetrexed and carboplatin was approved in China for the defined post-EGFR-TKI non-squamous NSCLC indication.

CURRENT STATE

SUPPORTED

SCOPE

Population-level regulatory status in China for EGFR-mutated locally advanced or metastatic non-squamous NSCLC progressing after EGFR-TKI; other populations, treatment lines, combinations, and jurisdictions are outside this claim.

EVIDENCE CUTOFF

2026-09-28

WHAT IS ESTABLISHED
  • An official NMPA public record supports approval of ivonescimab with pemetrexed and carboplatin for the defined China indication.
WHAT IS NOT ESTABLISHED
  • Approval for populations, treatment lines, combinations, or jurisdictions outside the regulator-defined scope
  • Comparative superiority or outcome claims beyond the cited evidence
WHAT WOULD CHANGE THIS

An official regulator record that changes, narrows, expands, suspends, withdraws, or corrects the approval status, label, combination, population, or treatment-line scope; or an authoritative correction or retraction of the cited regulatory source.

ACCEPTABLE SOURCES
  • NMPA approval label or official indication update
  • Official regulator safety, suspension, withdrawal, or correction notice
  • Authoritative source correction tied to the cited approval
NON-QUALIFYING SOURCES
  • Manufacturer commercial copy
  • Media summary without a regulator record
  • Trial result alone without a regulatory action
  • Approval in another jurisdiction
  • Unverified drug database entry
