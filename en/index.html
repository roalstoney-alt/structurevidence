Structural Opening

Find where an old advantage is weakening before the market fully reprices it.

A structural opening appears when a constraint, dependency, cost curve, regulation, or incumbent advantage begins to weaken. StructureEvidence does not call the opening a winner; it verifies whether the transition is real, bounded, and decision-relevant.
