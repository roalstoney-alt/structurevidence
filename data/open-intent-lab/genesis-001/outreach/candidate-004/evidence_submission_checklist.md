# Evidence submission checklist — OIL-CAND-004

For every item provide document title/type, issuer, responsible legal/manufacturing entity, exact BioPure product/part/size and revision, date, applicable Claim IDs, configuration/test conditions, supplier description, visibility choice, confidentiality restrictions, and public URL if available.

Choose one response per claim: `EVIDENCE_ATTACHED`, `PUBLIC_REFERENCE_PROVIDED`, `CLAIM_SCOPE_CORRECTION`, `CLAIM_WITHDRAWN`, `EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE`, `CONFIDENTIAL_ONLY`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

For pressure evidence, identify working pressure separately from proof or burst pressure and include the rating temperature and basis. Do not send pricing, customer identities, or unrelated validation packages. Raw hashing/ingestion occurs only in a later authorized stage.
