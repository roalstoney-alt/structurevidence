# Evidence submission checklist — OIL-CAND-002

For each evidence item provide:

- document title and type;
- issuer/accountable entity and manufacturing site where applicable;
- exact product family/series;
- date/revision;
- applicable frozen Claim ID(s);
- configuration, size, method, and conditions where relevant;
- supplier description;
- visibility choice: `PUBLIC`, `AUTHORIZED_BUYER`, or `TRANSACTION_PRIVATE`;
- confidentiality restrictions;
- public URL if available.

Select one response per claim: `EVIDENCE_ATTACHED`, `PUBLIC_REFERENCE_PROVIDED`, `CLAIM_SCOPE_CORRECTION`, `CLAIM_WITHDRAWN`, `EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE`, `CONFIDENTIAL_ONLY`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

Please keep SPT-70 FB IB, STHT-R, and any other product series separate. Raw hashing and ingestion occur only in a later authorized stage.
