# Evidence submission checklist — OIL-CAND-003

For every item provide document title/type, issuer, exact APSH-P/APSH product/revision mapping, date, applicable Claim IDs, configuration/test conditions, supplier description, visibility choice, confidentiality restrictions, and public URL if available.

Choose one response per claim: `EVIDENCE_ATTACHED`, `PUBLIC_REFERENCE_PROVIDED`, `CLAIM_SCOPE_CORRECTION`, `CLAIM_WITHDRAWN`, `EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE`, `CONFIDENTIAL_ONLY`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

Please identify whether the evidence applies to the current APSH-P series, an earlier APSH series, or both. Do not send pricing, customer identities, or unrelated validation packages. Raw hashing/ingestion occurs only in a later authorized stage.
