# Historical record notice — OIL-CAND-001

The purpose of this experiment is to create evidence-backed historical capability records.

If submitted evidence is later accepted through the defined review process, it is recorded with an as-of boundary, source, scope, and visibility. Future evidence or transaction outcomes may confirm, narrow, conflict with, supersede the scope of, or remain incomparable with earlier records. Earlier evidence is not silently deleted or rewritten solely because later information differs.

Material inconsistencies are preserved as part of the evidence history. A contradiction is recorded only after comparable product family, configuration, batch, factory, method, conditions, date, and source are examined. Materially different scope may be `NOT_COMPARABLE`.

Before the initial Genesis publication/freeze, the supplier may correct factual transcription, clarify scope, submit stronger evidence, or identify wrong entity attribution. This does not permit unsupported status changes.

Confidential evidence remains subject to the selected visibility boundary. Open Intent Lab does not create a universal supplier rating or paid ranking from these records.
