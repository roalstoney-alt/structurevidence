# Evidence submission checklist — OIL-CAND-001

For every submitted evidence item, please provide:

- document title;
- document type;
- issuer/accountable source;
- product or product family;
- document date/revision;
- applicable frozen Claim ID(s);
- exact scope, including size/configuration/test conditions where relevant;
- short supplier-provided description;
- requested visibility: `PUBLIC`, `AUTHORIZED_BUYER`, or `TRANSACTION_PRIVATE`;
- confidentiality restrictions;
- public reference URL, if available.

For each requested claim, select one response option:

- `EVIDENCE_ATTACHED`
- `PUBLIC_REFERENCE_PROVIDED`
- `CLAIM_SCOPE_CORRECTION`
- `CLAIM_WITHDRAWN`
- `EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE`
- `CONFIDENTIAL_ONLY`
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`

Do not send customer names, pricing, commercial terms, private drawings, capacity records, or batch records unless specifically relevant and deliberately marked `TRANSACTION_PRIVATE`. Raw file hashing and evidence ingestion occur only in a later authorized stage.
